
CUSTOMER SUPPORT BILLING POLICY

POLICY 1: DUPLICATE PAYMENTS
If a customer is charged twice for the same order,
the duplicate transaction must be verified.
Verified duplicate payments are eligible for a refund.

POLICY 2: REFUND ELIGIBILITY
A refund request must be checked against the
transaction record before approval.

POLICY 3: REFUND PROCESSING
Approved refunds are marked for processing.
The actual crediting time depends on the payment provider.

POLICY 4: FAILED PAYMENTS
If payment is deducted but the order is unsuccessful,
the transaction must be checked before determining
refund eligibility.

POLICY 5: INVOICE REQUESTS
Customers may request a copy of their invoice.
The support team must verify the order before providing it.

POLICY 6: HUMAN ESCALATION
If transaction information is unavailable or conflicting,
the case must be escalated to human billing support.

IMPORTANT:
Never claim that a refund has actually been processed
unless the transaction system confirms it.
